// projects

Fifteen apps, and the parts they share.

Most of this work is business software: dense forms, long lists, tricky permissions, and users who do the same task forty times a day. The interesting engineering is in what gets reused — one gym platform ships as four differently branded apps, and every ERP product sits on the same foundation.

// Technia · June 2024 – present

The Technia applications suite

Six products built on shared infrastructure. Each one gets its own domain model and workflows; none of them re-implements navigation, networking, theming, or state plumbing.

HR

Technia ESS

A legacy Flutter codebase refactored into scalable MVVM, then extended.

  • Attendance tracking and in-app clock in / clock out
  • Salary management
  • Request workflows end to end

Real estate

Technia Real Estate

A sales platform for property teams, tuned for long lists and frequent updates.

  • Leads, follow-ups, actions, and deals
  • Performance work on list rendering and state
  • Requirements gathered direct from clients

Fitness

Technia Gyms

Built from scratch, architected so new membership products drop in cleanly.

  • Packages and services
  • Memberships and subscriptions
  • Shipped as Passion Gym on both stores

Sales

Technia Sales

Reusable infrastructure carrying complex, modular sales workflows.

  • Leads and accounts
  • Work order management
  • Components shared back into the suite

Finance

Technia Accounting

Enterprise accounting where the data flow has to be exactly right.

  • Financial records and transactions
  • Structured reporting workflows
  • Validation close to the source

Procurement

Technia Purchasing

Supplier-facing procurement on the same clean architecture.

  • Suppliers and purchase orders
  • Approval chains
  • Inventory-related workflows
